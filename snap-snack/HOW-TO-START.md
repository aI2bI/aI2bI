# Snap Snack: how to start

Snap a photo of anything you eat. Snap Snack lists each item with calories, protein, carbs and fat, grades it A+ to F, and tracks your day, week and month.

**Your link:** https://claude.ai/artifact/QYsn9H9JtPMVYBz6EfCSJ1

## Setup (2 minutes)

1. **Tap the link.** It opens on its own. Don't paste it into a Claude chat.
2. **Sign in to Claude** when asked, or make an account at claude.ai. A free account may work; if photos don't work on free, tell me.
3. **Open the Targets tab** and fill in *Profile & goals*: first name, age, height, weight, goal weight. Tap **Suggest my targets**.
4. **Tap Snap** (orange button), take a picture of your food.
5. The first time, Claude asks permission to read your photos. **Tap Allow.**
6. Done. Every photo after that logs itself.

## Put it on your home screen

- **iPhone (Safari):** open the link → Share button → **Add to Home Screen**
- **Android (Chrome):** open the link → ⋮ menu → **Add to Home screen**

## Good to know

- **Your log is private.** It's saved to your own Claude account and syncs to any device where you sign in. Nobody else can see it, including whoever sent you the link.
- **Photos use your own Claude account's usage**, not anyone else's.
- **Fix anything by typing** in the bar at the bottom: "the rice was half a cup", "delete the cookie", "I walked 20 minutes", "my weight is 150", "undo".
- **Tap any photo** in the Gallery tab to see its full nutrition label and grade.
- **Tabs:** Today · Gallery (photo timeline) · Burn (calories burned by activity) · Progress (charts) · Week · Month · Targets.
- If the mic button doesn't appear, type your commands instead.
